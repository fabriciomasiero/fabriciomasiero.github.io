What it does

One focused tool for people who live on a Mac.

Not a team knowledge platform. Not a launcher. Not a clipboard suite. Snippr does one job: turn the things you type repeatedly into fast, reliable shortcuts — instantly, in every app.

⚡

Works in every app

Expansions fire in Mail, Slack, browsers, Xcode, Terminal, Notes, and any app that accepts typed text — no integration needed.

🍎

Built for macOS

A native SwiftUI menu-bar app with system styling, dark mode, categories, templates, usage stats, and launch at login.

↗

Smart triggers

Use any prefix like ; to avoid accidental expansions. Variable placeholders let you personalise text at expansion time.

📂

Organised categories

Group snippets by project, client, or topic. Add from templates or import from TextExpander in one click.

📊

Usage insights

See which snippets save you the most time, your daily streak, and a chart of when you type most — all on-device.

✦

Apple Intelligence

Improve or generate snippet text with on-device AI, and get abbreviation suggestions — no API key required.

Privacy first

No account. No cloud. No telemetry.

A text expander needs Accessibility permission to detect abbreviations. Snippr keeps that responsibility narrow: your snippets live on your Mac and nowhere else.

🔒

Local snippets

Everything — snippets, categories, usage counts, settings — is stored on your Mac with SwiftData. Nothing is uploaded.

🌐

Zero network

Snippr makes no network requests. No servers, no accounts, no telemetry, no analytics. It works fully offline.

🔑

One permission

Accessibility is used only to detect when you type an abbreviation. Raw keystrokes are never saved or transmitted.
