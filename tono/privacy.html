Privacy Policy

Tono is designed as a privacy-first, local-first photo editing app. Your photos stay yours, and core editing is designed to happen on-device.

Photos

Tono may request photo library access so you can import, edit, save, and share images. Core editing, Looks, rendering, basic analysis, and export are intended to run locally on your device.

Smart Recommendations

Tono may analyze brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, scene signals, faces or people, saliency, and other image statistics locally to recommend Looks and editing recipes.

Usage and Purchases

Tono may collect limited usage, diagnostics, purchase status, and technical information to improve the product, restore purchases, and unlock paid features. Usage analytics should not include your photo itself. Apple processes App Store payments and Tono does not receive your full payment card details.

What We Do Not Sell

Tono does not sell personal data, does not sell photos, and does not share photos with advertisers. Tono should not train external AI models on your personal photos without explicit permission.

Your Choices

You can limit or revoke photo access in iOS Settings, delete the app and local data, manage subscriptions through Apple ID settings, restore purchases, and contact support through the App Store listing.

Changes and Contact

This policy may be updated as Tono evolves. Material changes may be communicated through the app, App Store release notes, this website, or another reasonable method. For privacy questions, contact masiero572@gmail.com.
